Pizza

Description

Pizza is a baked flatbread topped with sauce and cheese.

It is one of the most popular foods worldwide.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Cheese
  • Pepperoni

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven.
  2. Roll out dough.
  3. Add sauce and toppings.
  4. Bake until ready.
