Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic layered pasta dish made with meat sauce and cheese.

It is rich, filling, and great for family meals.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna sheets
  • Minced beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven.
  2. Cook onion, garlic, and beef.
  3. Add tomato sauce and simmer.
  4. Layer pasta, sauce, and cheese.
  5. Bake until cooked through.
